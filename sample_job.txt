About the job
CLEEVEN is a European consulting firm founded on two fundamental convictions:
The excellence of European engineers must drive global competitiveness.
Our human mission is to support our consultants in their personal and professional development.


Context & Objectives :

Our client operates production machines powered by a highly customized legacy C++ application on Linux. The goal is to take ownership of the legacy code (reverse engineering), analyze/fix defects, deliver targeted enhancements, and secure maintainability (documentation, tests, tooling).


Key Responsibilities : 

Perform a technical audit of the codebase: module/dependency map, build chain, hot spots (concurrency, I/O, memory).
Conduct reverse engineering of critical components (code reading, tracing, instrumentation; disassembly if required).
Fix priority issues (stability, performance, leaks) and implement requested functional changes.
Produce and maintain technical documentation (README, install procedures, release notes) and ensure knowledge transfer to the client team.
Collaborate with OT/production for on-bench/line validation, deployment and rollback plans.


Profile :

2–3+ years with C++ on Linux
Systems background (processes, threads, synchronization, networking, files, serial) and performance tuning.
Comfortable with legacy code reading and incremental refactoring; reliability mindset in industrial environments.
Experience with static analysis (clang-tidy, cppcheck), Git, and CI (GitLab/Azure DevOps).
Nice to have: drivers/fieldbus (Modbus/CAN/OPC UA), Python/Bash scripting, cross-compilation, containers (Docker).
Industrial context (manufacturing/pharma/life sciences) and basic GxP/CSV awareness are appreciated.
English Mandatory / German is a huge plus
